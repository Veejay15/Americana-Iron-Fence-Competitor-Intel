## 5. Recommended Actions for Americana Iron Works

*These are combined recommended actions based on a review of all 3 competitor(s) monitored this week.*

1. Build a dedicated residential gates service page. Trigger: Innova Fence lost its ranking for "residential gates" entirely this week, a term with 320 monthly searches that was pointing to their /gates/ page. That position is now open and no competitor appears to own it cleanly. Why this fits Americana: Americana has gate installation pages organized by neighborhood but no standalone page targeting residential gates as a service category. Proposed URL slug: /custom-ironworks/residential-gate-installation. This topic does not already exist on Americana's site. The page should cover walk gates, driveway gates, and swing versus slide configurations for Chicago homes, and connect directly to Americana's existing neighborhood gate pages.

2. Build a project portfolio section with pages organized by job type. Trigger: Painters Inc launched five project portfolio pages under a /projects/ section in a single coordinated push, and simultaneously picked up an editorial backlink citing their commercial cost page as a real-world reference. The combination of visual proof of work and citable content drove meaningful ranking movement on high-volume terms. Why this fits Americana: Americana has a gallery page but no structured portfolio organized by service type. Proposed URL slugs: /portfolio/iron-fence-installation, /portfolio/custom-gate-installation, /portfolio/iron-railing-installation, /portfolio/fire-escape-installation. None of these exist on Americana's site. Each page should feature completed Chicago-area projects with photos, material details, and location context. This type of page earns citation links from local press, real estate agents, and design blogs the same way Painters Inc earned theirs.

3. Build a page targeting the frost line and fence post depth topic for Chicago. Trigger: Flores Fence is slipping on "frost line chicago," a 210-search informational term that attracts homeowners early in the planning process, and they have no dedicated page to defend it. Innova Fence has no foothold on this topic either. Why this fits Americana: Americana already has a blog post at /post/how-deep-should-fence-posts-be-in-chicago-frost-line-soil-and-post-depth-guide, which covers this territory. Update that existing page to target "frost line chicago" more directly in the title, headers, and opening paragraph. Add a clear answer up top, a simple depth chart by fence material, and a call to action tied to Americana's fence installation and repair services. The page exists and ranks for related terms. Strengthening it now while Flores Fence is sliding is a low-effort, high-timing move.
