**1. Reinforce the iron fence and ornamental metalwork pages with stronger calls to action and supporting content.**
Trigger: Flores Fence now has a published metal fence page and is ranking at position 1 for "fence company chicago" and "fence installation chicago," but nothing on their site addresses ornamental iron, custom gates, or railings. Why this fits Americana: Americana already has `/fence/iron-fence` and `/fence/wrought-iron-fence-installation`. Review those pages this week and make sure they clearly explain what separates custom ornamental ironwork from a standard metal fence. If a homeowner is choosing between a generic fence company and a custom iron works shop, those pages need to make the case.

**2. Confirm Americana's directory listings on Expertise.com (both the Chicago and Naperville fence company pages) are active, accurate, and link back to the site.**
Trigger: Expertise.com links are among Flores Fence's most authoritative active citations, including one from the Naperville fence companies listing. Why this fits Americana: Americana already serves the North Shore and suburbs. If Flores is appearing in the Naperville Expertise.com roundup and Americana is not, that is a local citation gap worth closing this week. Check Expertise.com, Yellow Pages, and BestProsInTown as a set.

**3. Publish a dedicated page targeting chain link fence installation in Chicago, if Americana installs chain link fences.**
Trigger: Flores Fence published `/chain-fence` this week and already has a ranking position for vinyl fence. They are building out material-specific service pages. Why this fits Americana: Americana already has `/fence/chain-link-fence`. Check that page and confirm it is optimized for Chicago-specific searches ("chain link fence Chicago," "chain link fence installation Chicago"). If the page is thin, add more detail about commercial and residential applications, Americana's service area, and a clear quote request path. **4. Check whether Americana has a dedicated page for wood fence installation and, if not, evaluate whether it belongs in the service mix.**
Trigger: Flores Fence published `/wood-fence` this week, adding it alongside vinyl, metal, and chain link as a material-specific service page. If it is currently a thin page, this is the week to expand it, given Flores is actively building out competing content.

**5. Do not attempt to match Flores Fence on generic "fence company Chicago" terms head to head with homepage optimization alone.**
Flores holds position 1 for multiple high-volume generic fence terms and their homepage is the ranking page. Americana's path to visibility on those terms runs through service-specific and location-specific pages, not homepage keyword stuffing. The more productive focus this week is the territory Flores is not contesting: iron gates, custom railings, fire escapes, and ornamental ironwork for both residential and commercial properties. Those pages already exist on Americana's site. Make sure they are the strongest versions they can be.
