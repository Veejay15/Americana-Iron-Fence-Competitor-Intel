Why this fits Americana: wrought iron and ornamental iron fencing for residential clients is Americana's core identity. A focused page on this topic, built with real specificity about Chicago neighborhoods, custom work, and iron over generic metal alternatives, would outperform a fence company using a catch-all metal fence page.

Why this fits Americana: a dedicated location-and-service landing page built specifically around this keyword cluster, rather than relying on the homepage to do that work, gives Americana a more targeted answer to these searches and an opportunity to rank where both competitors are currently competing with diluted homepage authority.
